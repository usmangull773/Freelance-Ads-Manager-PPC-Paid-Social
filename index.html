What we found

Your post is not really asking for someone to run ads. It is asking for consistency across a growing roster: the same quality on account twelve as on account one, reports in your format, and problems flagged to the right person before a client notices them.

Quality drifts as the account count grows

High impact

With multiple freelancers on a roster, the usual failure is not skill. It is that each person checks different things on different days. The fix is one fixed checklist and cadence per account, so every account gets the same review whether it spends $500 or $15,000 a month.

1
Week 1

Access, audit and tracking verification

Get access to the first accounts you assign. Check every conversion action in Google Ads, Meta pixel and CAPI events, and GA4 links. List anything broken before touching bids or budgets.

Why: optimizing on wrong conversion data makes every later decision wrong.

2
Week 1 to 2

Lock your report format and cadence

Use your template exactly. If you do not have one yet, I bring a one page weekly update (sample below) and we adjust it once, then it stays fixed.

Why: account managers should never have to rewrite an update before it goes to a client.

3
Week 2 to 4

Run the same optimization routine on every account

Pacing twice a week. Search terms and negatives weekly. Bid strategy and budget moves toward the campaigns and ad groups producing the cheapest qualified leads. Audience and creative checks on Meta.

Why: a fixed routine is what keeps account twelve as clean as account one.

4
Week 3 to 4

Route flags to creative, web and media

Each flag names the account, the metric, the trend and the owner. Urgent issues, like a tracking break or overspend, go out the same day, not in the weekly update.

Why: you asked for someone who tells you when something is off. This makes it a habit, not a favor.

5
Week 4

Review volume and document the workflow for your platform

We agree how many more accounts to add. I hand over the routine as a written checklist so your platform team can turn it into statuses and alerts.

Why: your system gets built on how the work is actually done.
